# Windows checklist

The desktop app was built and tested on Linux and in a browser. These are the
things that could only be checked on a real Windows 11 PC. Go down the list
and note anything that does not match.

## Install

1. The installer runs after "More info" then "Run anyway".
2. The star appears near the bottom right corner, above the taskbar.
3. There is no box, border or shadow around the star: only the star and its
   glow, with your desktop showing through around it.

## Clicks and the mouse

4. Clicking right next to the star (but not on it) clicks whatever is behind
   it, for example a desktop icon.
5. Clicking on the star opens the list. Clicking it again closes it.
6. You can drag the star anywhere, and it stays there after you quit and
   start the app again.
7. The eyes look towards the mouse as it moves around the screen.
8. Moving the mouse over the star shows its speech bubble.
9. **Typing test.** Open Notepad and type. Now move the mouse over the star
   and away again, without clicking, and keep typing. The letters should
   still go into Notepad.

## Quick add and the tray

10. Ctrl+Shift+A opens the quick add box from any program. Typing
    "maths hw fri" and pressing Enter adds it, and the star says "Added."
11. There is a star icon near the clock (maybe under the small arrow). Right
    click shows the menu, left click opens the list.
12. "Start when I log in" in the menu: turn it on, restart the PC, and the
    star comes back by itself.

## Full screen

13. Play a YouTube video and make it full screen. Within about 2 seconds the
    star disappears.
14. Leave full screen. The star comes back in the same place, and the space
    bar still pauses the video without clicking on the browser first.
15. Start a game in full screen: the star is gone. Leave the game: it is back.
16. A maximised window (not full screen) does not hide the star.

## Focus timer

17. Click the star, press "Focus" on a task. The star turns green and the
    list shows a countdown.
18. When the time is up you hear two soft notes, the star celebrates, and
    the session shows up as done.

## While it sits there

19. Open Task Manager, find Star Companion, and watch it for a minute while
    you do nothing. CPU should read 0% almost all the time.

If something is wrong, tell me the number and what you saw.
