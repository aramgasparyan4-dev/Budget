## Recording and editing (phone + CapCut)

1. **Record:** use a quiet, soft room (a wardrobe full of clothes works well). Hold the phone about 15 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side. Use the Voice Memos app or CapCut's *Voiceover* button.
2. **Easiest method:** in CapCut, import `cooldown_tiktok_60s_no_voice.mp4`, put headphones on, press *Voiceover*, and read along with the captions in one take. Re-record any line that drifts.
3. **Line by line:** record each line separately and drag each one so it starts at the time in the **Start** column.
4. **Levels:** leave the video's own audio at 100%. Set your voice so it's clearly on top, which is usually 100–150% in CapCut. The music is already lowered under every line.
5. **Export:** 1080p, 30 fps.
