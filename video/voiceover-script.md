# Voiceover script: *Cooldown* (60 s)

## How to record (the easy way)

1. Put **headphones** on, so the music doesn't leak into your recording.
2. Start a voice recording on your phone (Voice Memos or any recorder app). Keep it close to your mouth, in a quiet, soft room.
3. Play **`out/voiceover_read_along.mp4`** on your laptop. It counts you in with **3, 2, 1** beeps. Each word on the right lights up exactly when you should say it. The status chip tells you **GET READY**, **SPEAK NOW** or **PAUSE**.
4. Read the whole minute in one take, then stop recording after the music ends.
5. Send me the audio file (any format: m4a, mp3, wav). I'll line it up with the video, re-time the captions to your voice, re-balance the music under you, and rebuild all the versions.

Want to rehearse first? **`out/voiceover_read_along_practice.mp4`** is identical but includes the AI guide voice, so you can hear the pace.

Keep the words **exactly as written**, because the captions are rebuilt from this script. Small timing differences are fine; I fix those when I integrate your recording.

**Pace:** quick, upbeat and conversational, about 3 words per second. Think of talking to a friend, not reading an advert.
