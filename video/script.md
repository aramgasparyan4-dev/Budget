# Video script and storyboard: *Cooldown* (60 s, 9:16)

Final file: [`out/cooldown_tiktok_60s.mp4`](out/cooldown_tiktok_60s.mp4) (1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, −14 LUFS). For projecting on stage: [`out/cooldown_presentation_16x9.mp4`](out/cooldown_presentation_16x9.mp4) (1920×1080).
Cover: [`out/cover.jpg`](out/cover.jpg). Subtitles: [`out/captions.srt`](out/captions.srt).

**Visual idea:** a printed-paper editorial look rather than a glossy tech ad. The palette is warm paper, ink black, **tomato** for the impulse half and **cobalt** for Cooldown. Type is Fraunces (soft, slightly wonky serif) for headlines, Bricolage Grotesque for UI and captions, real pen handwriting (Nanum Pen Script) for notes, and Courier Prime on receipts. Graphics are flat ink-outlined phones with hard printed shadows, hand-drawn circles, arrows and strike-throughs, torn-paper scene changes and a light paper grain. There are no emoji, glows or gradients. The story still moves from **hot to cool**: the tomato posters give way to a cobalt ink blot at 0:14. It follows one person, Maya: she's stopped at a 1 a.m. checkout, and three months later the money she *didn't* spend becomes a flight to Tokyo.

## How it covers the brief

| Brief requirement | Where |
|---|---|
| Strong opening hook | Frame 1 already says **STOP.** The video speaks directly to the viewer ("Don't tap buy now") on TikTok, the home of #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt, and freezes on a cliffhanger. |
| Clear visuals | A distinctive printed-paper style (tomato/cobalt posters, hand-drawn annotations, receipts and stamps) rather than a stock tech look. One continuous phone UI shows the full user journey, with the camera zooming in on each key number. Highlighter-style karaoke captions for sound-off viewing, everything inside TikTok's safe zones. |
| Simple explanation of benefits | Three steps (real price → 24 h cooldown → auto-save) and four benefit stickers. |
| Suitable music / trends | Original 120 BPM future-house track that drops on the reveal, with sound effects synced to every action. The #UnspentChallenge plays on the "underconsumption" / anti-haul trend. |
| Direct call to action | Join the free waitlist, link in bio (on screen and in the voiceover), plus a "tap follow" loop line. |
| 1-minute vertical video made with AI tools | 60.0 s, 1080×1920. AI voice, AI-written script and AI-built animation (see [ai-prompts.md](ai-prompts.md)). |
