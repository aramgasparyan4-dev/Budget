Final file: [`out/cooldown_tiktok_60s.mp4`](out/cooldown_tiktok_60s.mp4) (1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, −14 LUFS).

**Visual idea:** the video moves from **hot to cool**. The problem scenes are red and orange ("hot" impulse state). A frost wipe at 0:14 turns everything icy blue as the product arrives ("cool" state). The colour change *is* the value proposition.

## How it covers the brief

| Brief requirement | Where |
|---|---|
| Strong opening hook | Frame 1 already says **STOP.** The video speaks directly to the viewer ("Don't tap buy now") on TikTok, the home of #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt, and freezes on a cliffhanger. |
| Clear visuals | One continuous phone UI shows the full user journey. Hot/cool colour coding, karaoke captions for sound-off viewing, everything inside TikTok's safe zones. |
| Simple explanation of benefits | Three steps (real price → 24 h cooldown → auto-save) and four benefit stickers. |
| Suitable music / trends | Original 120 BPM future-house track that drops on the reveal, with sound effects synced to every action. The #UnspentChallenge plays on the "underconsumption" / anti-haul trend. |
| Direct call to action | Join the free waitlist, link in bio (on screen and in the voiceover), plus a "tap follow" loop line. |
| 1-minute vertical video made with AI tools | 60.0 s, 1080×1920. AI voice, AI-written script and AI-built animation (see [ai-prompts.md](ai-prompts.md)). |
